# BION galactic tests — reproducibility status

The journal-style TD3R synthesis treats the current BION/SPARC calculations as exploratory, not as a validated observational result.

The complete raw input/output archive of the earliest pilot tests is not available in the present release. For that reason, no numerical claim in the synthesis article depends on those pilots as a closed result.

A publication-grade rerun should:

1. freeze a dated copy of the official SPARC tables and their provenance;
2. archive the exact sample selection and quality cuts;
3. derive the TD3R mapping before opening the validation/test subset;
4. record all global parameters, nuisance parameters and random seeds where relevant;
5. archive scripts and machine-readable outputs;
6. evaluate individual rotation curves together with RAR, BTFR, diversity and lensing observables.

Official SPARC portal used by the article: https://astroweb.cwru.edu/SPARC/

Primary reference: F. Lelli, S. S. McGaugh and J. M. Schombert, *Astronomical Journal* **152**, 157 (2016), DOI `10.3847/0004-6256/152/6/157`.
